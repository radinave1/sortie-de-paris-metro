Sortie de métro Paris — metroExpert

Sources

Transport information comes from Île-de-France Mobilités open data:

  • Station entrances (acces, relations-acces) — Licence Ouverte v2.0 (Etalab).
  • GTFS timetables — Licence Mobilités.
  • RATP car positions for transfers and exits, published by Île-de-France Mobilités — Licence Ouverte v2.0 (Etalab). Source last updated: 18 February 2025.

Exit positions were checked against data from © OpenStreetMap contributors, available under the Open Database License (ODbL).

Network and exits bundled on 9 October 2026. Car-position source update: 18 February 2025. Journeys are theoretical and do not include live disruptions or departures.

Derived dataset

The dataset bundled in the app is a derived database. It is made available under the Open Database License (ODbL) v1.0: metro-exit-guide.json. The separately bundled official RATP car-position data is available as ratp-positions.json under Licence Ouverte v2.0.

Sortie de métro Paris is an independent app and is not an official RATP or Île-de-France Mobilités application.
