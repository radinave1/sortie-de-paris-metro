Terms of Service

Usage and subscription terms

Service

The app provides guidance to help riders choose a train position near useful metro exits.

Recommendations are informational and may be incomplete or incorrect when station layouts, public data, construction, or train operations change.

Subscription

Access to the guide requires an active free trial or active subscription.

The monthly plan starts with 1 month free for eligible new subscribers, then renews automatically at EUR 1/month unless cancelled before renewal in Google Play.

If the free trial ends and the subscription is not active, app access is locked until a subscription is started, renewed, or restored.

You can cancel or manage the subscription from Google Play. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

Branding

sortie de metro paris is an independent app and is not an official RATP or Ile-de-France Mobilites application unless a future authorized partnership is stated clearly.
