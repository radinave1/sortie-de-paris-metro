Terms of Service

Usage and subscription terms

Service

The app provides guidance to help riders choose a train position near useful metro exits.

Recommendations are informational and may be incomplete or incorrect when station layouts, public data, construction, or train operations change.

Subscription

The direct-trip guide is free. Premium features (favorite trips, recent trips and the exit map) require an active free trial or active subscription.

The monthly plan includes a 3-month free trial for eligible new subscribers. It then renews automatically each month at the price shown by Google Play before confirmation, unless cancelled before renewal. Prices and offer eligibility may vary by country and account.

If the free trial ends and the subscription is not active, Premium features are locked until a subscription is started, renewed, or restored. The direct-trip guide stays available.

You can cancel or manage the subscription from Google Play. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

Branding

sortie de metro paris is an independent app and is not an official RATP or Ile-de-France Mobilites application unless a future authorized partnership is stated clearly.
