Privacy Policy

Sortie de métro Paris — published on Google Play by metroExpert

Overview

sortie de metro paris is designed to work offline with a bundled Paris metro exit dataset.

The guide does not require an account, location tracking, contacts, photos, microphone access, camera access, or advertising identifiers.

Data used by the app

The app stores your current in-app selections on your device while you use the guide.

The bundled dataset includes public transport and station-exit information. It is not personal user data.

Purchases

If you subscribe, Google Play processes the payment and manages cancellation, renewal, refunds, and purchase status.

RevenueCat is used to validate subscription entitlement and restore purchases. It processes purchase history, purchase identifiers, subscription status and an anonymous app user identifier to validate access, restore purchases and provide subscription analytics. Technical app and platform information accompanies subscription requests. The identifier is not an advertising identifier. The app does not receive card or bank details.

Service providers and security

RevenueCat Inc. processes subscription data as our service provider. We do not sell it or use it for advertising. Data sent to RevenueCat is encrypted in transit. The app has no separate crash-reporting or advertising service. Station, destination and exit selections are not sent to RevenueCat or to us. Learn more in RevenueCat's Privacy Policy and Google's Privacy Policy.

Support requests

If you email our support address, we use your email address and the information you provide to answer your request. Do not send passwords, payment card details or unnecessary personal information.

Retention and deletion

Selections used by the guide remain on your device. Clearing the app's storage or uninstalling removes its local data. Subscription records are retained as needed to operate and restore access and resolve support, purchase or refund requests, unless you request deletion of data we can delete.

You can request deletion of subscription data held by RevenueCat and support correspondence by emailing radinave@gmail.com. Requests are handled within 30 days. To locate an anonymous subscription record, we may ask for the relevant Google Play order reference; we do not automatically receive your Google email address. See Data Deletion Request for the steps.

Deleting app or RevenueCat data does not cancel a subscription. Cancel it separately in Google Play subscriptions. Google controls its own payment records and retention obligations; deleting our data does not delete Google's records.

Contact

For privacy or support requests, contact radinave@gmail.com.
