Privacy Policy

Data practices for sortie de metro paris

Overview

sortie de metro paris is designed to work offline with a bundled Paris metro exit dataset.

The guide does not require an account, location tracking, contacts, photos, microphone access, camera access, or advertising identifiers.

Data used by the app

The app stores your current in-app selections on your device while you use the guide.

The bundled dataset includes public transport and station-exit information. It is not personal user data.

Purchases

If you subscribe, Google Play processes the payment and manages cancellation, renewal, refunds, and purchase status.

RevenueCat is used to validate subscription entitlement and restore purchases. It may process purchase identifiers, subscription status, device/app identifiers needed for entitlement, and diagnostics needed to operate subscriptions.

Data we declare on Google Play Data Safety

Consistent with the above, the only data collected by this app via third parties is: purchase history, an app-scoped install identifier, and diagnostics — all collected by RevenueCat to operate the subscription. Data in transit is encrypted with TLS. You can request deletion of subscription-linked data at any time by emailing radinave@gmail.com.

Contact

For privacy or support requests, contact radinave@gmail.com.
