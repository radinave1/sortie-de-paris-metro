Data Deletion Request

Sortie de métro Paris — metroExpert

No app account is required

The app does not offer account creation. You can still request deletion of subscription data processed through RevenueCat and correspondence sent to our support address.

Request deletion

  1. Email radinave@gmail.com with the subject “Data deletion request — Sortie de métro Paris”.
  2. Tell us whether you want subscription data, support correspondence, or both deleted. If you made a purchase, include its Google Play order reference (GPA…) so we can locate the anonymous subscription record. We may request a receipt with unrelated personal information removed to verify your request.
  3. We will reply and process the request within 30 days. Never send your Google password or bank/card details.

Data deleted and data retained

We delete the matching RevenueCat customer record, including its associated purchase and entitlement data and anonymous identifier, and/or the support correspondence you request. Google's own purchase and payment records are controlled by Google and are not deleted by this request.

Local data and subscriptions

To remove local app data, use Android Settings → Apps → Sortie de métro Paris → Storage → Clear storage, or uninstall the app.

Deleting data or uninstalling does not cancel billing and can remove your access to the guide. If you want to stop renewal, cancel separately in Google Play subscriptions. Restoring purchases or making a new purchase can create a new RevenueCat record.
